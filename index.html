Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy covers two related Android apps. Both are personal projects, written for one household and one piece of hardware.

  • Alfred for Car, package com.samseide.alfredcar, the app published on Google Play.
  • Meta Bridge, package com.samseide.metabridge, a companion app distributed privately. It is not published on Google Play.

Both apps talk to an Alfred relay: a small service that runs on a computer owned and operated by the same person who writes the apps. Neither app includes a relay.

Summary

Neither app collects, transmits, sells, or shares personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Alfred for Car

What it does

It shows a single screen in your car through Android Auto. You tap the button, speak, and tap again to stop. The recording is sent to the relay address you configure. The spoken reply comes back and plays through the car speakers.

What is collected

Nothing is collected by the developer.

The app records audio only while you hold the talk button open. That recording is sent to the relay address you entered and to no other destination. No copy of it is sent to the developer. The only thing the app stores on your phone is the relay address you typed, kept in the app's own private settings. It holds no personal information, and you can change or clear it at any time from the app's settings screen.

Permissions and why they exist

  • Microphone: records your voice while the talk button is held.
  • Internet and network state: reaches the relay address you configured.
  • Foreground service and foreground service (microphone): keeps the voice session alive while the app is in use.

The published app requests no other permissions. It does not request access to your contacts, location, text messages, phone state, physical activity, Bluetooth devices, or notifications.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No user accounts and no sign-in.
  • No collection of device identifiers, location, or usage data.
  • No reading or sending of text messages.
  • No sharing with third parties, because nothing is collected to share.

Data storage

Configuration is stored locally on your device only. It is not backed up to, or retrievable by, the developer.

Meta Bridge (companion app, not published on Google Play)

Meta Bridge is a companion utility for the same relay, used with personal robotics hardware. It collects no personal data either.

  • Internet and local network access: communicates with the relay on your network, or at a relay address you configure yourself.
  • Bluetooth, if granted: connects to paired audio and wearable devices you choose. No data is transmitted off-device.
  • Microphone, if granted: captures voice commands for your own relay. Audio is not stored, uploaded, or shared by the app.

Permissions are used only for the functions listed above. No permission is used to profile you, and none is used to send data to the developer or to anyone else.

Children's privacy

Neither app is directed at children, and neither collects data from anyone, including children under 13.
