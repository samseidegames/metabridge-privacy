Privacy Policy — Meta Bridge

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Meta Bridge (“the app”, package com.samseide.metabridge) is a companion utility for the Meta Bridge relay used with personal robotics hardware. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Summary

Meta Bridge does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Information We Collect

None. The app does not collect personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, or usage statistics.

Permissions and Why They Exist

  • Network / Internet access — used solely to communicate with the Meta Bridge relay on your local network or a relay address you configure yourself.
  • Local network access — required to reach the relay device on your LAN.
  • Bluetooth (if granted) — used only to connect to paired audio/wearable devices you choose; no data is transmitted off-device.
  • Microphone (if granted) — used only for on-device voice command capture routed to your own relay. Audio is not stored, uploaded, or shared by the app.

Permissions are used only for the functionality above. No permission is used to profile you or to send data to us or to anyone else.

Data Storage

Any configuration you enter (relay address, device names, preferences) is stored locally on your device only. It is not backed up to, or retrievable by, us.

Data Sharing

We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any.

Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised “last updated” date.
